Platform capabilities

Everything a school needs to run admissions online.

From leadership verification to automated receipts — one secure system that replaces queues, paper registers and manual bank slips.

Instant form sales

Publish joining forms, interview forms and joining instructions. Parents pay and download immediately — no office visit required.

Verified leadership roles

Register the Head Teacher, Deputy, School Manager and Academic Master/Mistress with names, phone numbers and emails.

Google & phone sign-in

Guardians authenticate with a Google account or a verified phone number and one-time code. No passwords to remember.

Child-linked purchases

Guardians purchasing for a child enter the child's full name and class — the school receives a clean, class-organised list.

Mobile money & card

MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, Visa and Mastercard. Funds settle to the school wallet with automatic receipts for every order.

Live analytics & payouts

Track sales by form, class and date. Export applicant lists as CSV and request withdrawals to your school bank account.
